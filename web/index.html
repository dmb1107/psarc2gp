      Setup and help

      How to use tab2gp

      1. Get songs. Rocksmith custom songs (.psarc) from CustomsForge, or tabs from Songsterr with the bookmark below.
      2. Add them. Drop .psarc files or a whole folder on the library page (or use its buttons). Each song becomes a Guitar Pro 8 file with the original recording and a sync point on every beat.
      3. Practise. Click a song to open the player: follow the tab with the recording, slow it down, drag across bars to loop a passage, or switch to the synthesizer to hear the notes alone.
      4. Take it to Guitar Pro. The download button gives you the .gp file; open it in Guitar Pro 8 to edit, with the recording still in sync.

      Your files stay on this computer. Conversion runs inside this page and songs are kept in this browser's storage; nothing is uploaded. Download the songs you want to keep for good.

      Songsterr: 1. The shared audio key (optional)

      tab2gp gets a Songsterr video's audio from a conversion service, with a key shared by a few friends and locked with a password. Everything else works without it: you can always use your own audio file of the video instead.

      Unlocked in this browser.

      This copy of tab2gp has no shared audio key: songs need your own audio file.

      Songsterr: 2. The bookmark

      1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar: Open in tab2gp

      2. Open a song on songsterr.com and wait until its tab has loaded.
      3. Click the bookmark. tab2gp opens with the song: pick the tracks and the video, and it becomes a Guitar Pro file with the video's audio, synced bar by bar to Songsterr's own timings.

      The bookmark only reads what the Songsterr page has already loaded, and hands it to tab2gp. If the handover doesn't work, its panel saves the song as a file instead: (or drop it on the library page).
